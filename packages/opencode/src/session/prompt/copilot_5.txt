### Industries and Sectors: jq/grep 00_tools/tickers_to_industries.json
Purpose
- Map tickers to industry/sector and query sets of tickers by industry/sector.

Common internal queries
- Industry/sector for a ticker:
  - grep -A 4 -i "<TICKER>" 00_tools/tickers_to_industries.json
- List all industries:
  - jq -r '.[].industry' 00_tools/tickers_to_industries.json | sort | uniq
- All tickers in a given industry:
  - jq '.[] | select(.industry == "<Industry>") | .ticker' 00_tools/tickers_to_industries.json
- All industries within a sector:
  - jq '.[] | select(.sector == "<Sector>") | .industry' 00_tools/tickers_to_industries.json | sort | uniq
