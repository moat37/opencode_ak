# Frameworks To Use

### Extended Business Model Canvas
When a query is asking to know more about a business or its business model, use the below extended business model canvas framework to formulate your answer.
The Extended Business Model Canvas framework includes Potential Economic Moats:
Customer Segments, Revenue Streams, Value Proposition, Potential Economic Moats, Cost Structure, Channels, Customer Relationships, Key Resources, Key Activites, and Key Partnerships.

### Porter's Five Forces
When a query involves understanding the competitive forces at play, use Porter's Five Forces to formulate your answer. However, don't assume the user knows what "Porter's Five Forces" means — introduce it in 10-12 words.

### Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats
When assessing a company's current situation and potential future prospects, use the SWOT framework to structure your answer. Avoid using the acronym SWOT, just use the words Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats directly.

### PESTEL Framework
 When assessing the external macro-environment factors that can impact an organization, use the PESTEL framework. Avoid using the acronym PESTEL, just use the words Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal factors directly.

### Culture, Talent and Tech-Savviness
If the company is in a new-age industry (such as tech and knowledge work), take every opportunity to gather information about the culture of the company (using 00_tools/annual_report_reader.py). This includes an emphasis on fostering, attracting and retaining talent. It also includes learning and development initiatives especially in terms of technology or innovation.

### Framework for Assessing Corporate Governance
Use this framework to assess the corporate governance standard of a business.
1. **Board Structure and Effectiveness**
   - **Composition**: Is the board diverse (skills, experience, gender, independence)? Are there enough independent directors (e.g., at least 50% in many standards)?  
   - **Roles and Responsibilities**: Are the roles of Chair and CEO/MD separated? Does the board set strategy, oversee management, and monitor risk?  
   - **Committees**: Are there independent audit, nomination, and remuneration committees with clear charters?  
   - **Evaluation**: Is there a regular board performance evaluation process?  
   - **Assessment Metrics**: Board meeting frequency, attendance, director qualifications, and conflict-of-interest policies.
2. **Transparency and Disclosure**
   - **Financial Reporting**: Are financial statements timely, accurate, and compliant with standards (e.g., Ind-AS)?
   - **Non-Financial Reporting**: Does the company disclose its business risks satisfactorily?
   - **Communication**: Are disclosures clear, accessible, and consistent for shareholders and stakeholders?
   - **Assessment Metrics**: Quality of annual reports, frequency of updates, and external audit credibility.
3. **Executive Compensation and Alignment**
   - **Pay Structure**: Is executive pay tied to performance (e.g., long-term incentives)?  
   - **Transparency**: Are compensation policies disclosed and justified?
   - **Assessment Metrics**: Pay-performance linkage, shareholder approval of pay packages, and clawback provisions.
4. **Stakeholder Relations**
   - **Engagement**: Does the company consider the interests of minority shareholders? The answer to this is likely found in concall documents and the actions of the management (actions speak louder than words).
   - **Reputation**: Does the company maintain trust through ethical practices?  
   - **Assessment Metrics**: Actions of the management over a 3-5 years period and interactions in the concalls documents.
5. **Legal and Regulatory Compliance**
   - **Regulatory Adherence**: Does the company have any current or historical trouble with SEBI / RBI or any other regulatory body?
   - **Legal Adherence**: Does the company have any major current or historical legal liabilities filed against them?
   - **Assessment Metrics**: Regulatory fines, lawsuits, or governance-related sanctions.
6. **Risk Management and Internal Controls**
   - **Risk Oversight**: Does the board oversee a robust risk management framework (e.g., financial, operational, cyber, reputational risks)?
   - **Internal Controls**: Are there strong internal audit processes and compliance systems?
   - **Ethics and Compliance if it's a large organisation**: Is there a code of conduct, whistleblower policy, and anti-corruption measures?
   - **Assessment Metrics**: Risk reports, audit findings, and compliance violations.
